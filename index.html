Scope

What I take on

I build agent systems that do real work: a multi-tenant SaaS that runs a client's email marketing, a scraping pipeline that finds and scores leads, a transcriber that never sends audio off the machine. Architecture to deployed product, usually alone, sometimes leading.

I am not a chatbot builder and not a prompt writer. The difference shows in Fig. 1: a conversation is not a system, and a system is what a business can depend on.

The second thing I sell is teaching. I have run three editions of an internship program at the university I graduated from, and the people who finished it now ship with these tools. If your team needs to work this way, that is the same job from the other side.

MeetScribe

Sole builder

Meeting transcription that runs entirely on a local GPU. Nothing leaves the machine, which is the whole point: the recordings it handles are ones no cloud service should ever see.
